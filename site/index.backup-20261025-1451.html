Questions

Good to know before you book.

Where is Deluxe Car Wash & SPA?

We are at Strovolou 196, Strovolos 2048, in central Nicosia, Cyprus. Call +357 22 08 08 00 or message us on WhatsApp at +357 97 424020.

How much does ceramic coating cost in Nicosia?

At Deluxe, ceramic coating starts from €400 for 1-year protection, €600 for 3-year and €800 for 5-year, for a Small vehicle. Larger vehicles are quoted at booking. Glass and leather-seat ceramic add-ons start from €50 and €80.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking online takes about two minutes and guarantees your slot. Walk-ins are welcome when there is space, but detailing and coating work is best booked ahead.

How do I pay?

You book your service and time online, then pay at the wash when the work is done.

What is the difference between a wash and detailing?

A wash cleans the surface. Detailing restores and protects: paint decontamination and polishing, deep interior cleaning, and optional ceramic coating that keeps the car cleaner and glossier for years.
